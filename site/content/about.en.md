## Why we build it

Family money is usually scattered: one person has a spreadsheet, another keeps notes on their phone, a third has receipts in a wallet. We are building one place where the family sees the shared budget while personal stays personal. Logging an expense takes 5 seconds — or just tell Claude, or send it a photo of the receipt.

## What matters to us

- **Together, with boundaries.** A shared family project and personal projects, with owner, editor and viewer roles.
- **Three languages.** Everything — the app, the site, guides and news — is in Russian, English and Thai. We live between these languages ourselves.
- **Privacy.** No ads and no trackers. Passwords are kept by a separate sign-in server, backups are encrypted, and you can delete your data at any time.
- **Honest accounting.** Double-entry under the hood: transfers and currency exchange between accounts don't distort spending, and balances match the bank.
- **Openness.** The source code is open on [GitHub](https://github.com/Yolshin195/family-budget), and here on the [blog](@/blog/_index.en.md) we tell you what has changed and what we plan.
