With this version we start "What's new": a short story about every release will appear here. Here is what the app can do today.

## New

- **Shared and personal budgets.** Projects with members and owner, editor and viewer roles. Invite your family with a link or a QR code.
- **An expense in 5 seconds** from any page: amount, category, Enter. Frequent categories at hand, "120+45" adds up by itself, and on a computer there is the <kbd>N</kbd> key.
- **Accounts, transfers and currency exchange** in a single transaction, with a fee and at the real rate. Double-entry under the hood keeps balances in line with the bank.
- **Limits across the category tree** and a weekly, monthly and yearly budget: actual, plan and forecast, with a warning at 80% and when you go over.
- **A calendar of planned payments**: rent, subscriptions, salary. "Pay" is a single tap.
- **Claude keeps the budget for you.** Connect Claude to your budget and log spending by voice or by a receipt photo right from the chat. You decide what the assistant may do; nothing can be deleted through it. How to connect — in the [guide](@/guides/connect-claude/index.en.md).
- **Three languages**: Russian, English and Thai — the interface, categories and transaction descriptions.

## Recent improvements

- A new budget comes with a "Cash" account in the base currency, so you can log the first expense without any setup.
- The calendar shows amounts in the base currency, and the Overview switches between "Actual" and "Forecast" modes.
- An exchange rate can be entered either way: "1 USD = 36.5 ฿" or "1 ฿ = 2.5 ₽" — whichever you are used to.

You can try all of this without signing up in the demo: the "View the demo" button on the home page.
