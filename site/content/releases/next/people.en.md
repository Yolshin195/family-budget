“Directories” now have “People”: project members are added automatically, and you can add a child or a nanny without an account. In the full transaction details, under “More”, there are “For whom by default” and “Different recipients in one receipt”: the paints from a shared receipt can go to your daughter while the rest stays “For everyone”. “Transactions” shows who a purchase was for, and the “For whom” filter shows how much was spent on each person. Quick entry hasn't changed — a purchase is “For everyone” by default. Claude sets the recipient too, and adds new people only with your consent and the “People: add and edit” permission. More in the guide [“People and “For whom””](@/guides/people/index.en.md).
