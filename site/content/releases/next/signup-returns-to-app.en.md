Create a budget no longer ends with an error at the sign-up step. Afterwards the app shows Check your email with three steps: the email, a password via the link, then Sign in. A family invitation is kept even if the email opens in another browser. If signing in takes too long while you wait for the email, it restarts on its own, and a sign-in started in another browser gets a clear hint.
