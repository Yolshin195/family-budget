In Safari on iPhone and Mac, the "Expense", "+" and transfer buttons and the round "+" at the bottom of the screen stopped opening the quick entry form — Chrome was fine. Now the form opens everywhere, and in Safari instantly too: its data is loaded in advance, as in Chrome. Also, in the full transaction form the "Account" field shows the account name again instead of just the currency symbol, and "Total" shows the currency.
