You can now keep the budget through ChatGPT as well. The Assistants (MCP) page has a "Claude | ChatGPT" switch: each assistant has its own project and permissions, and "Revoke" closes access for that assistant only. If access is not granted yet, the assistant gives a link straight to its own tab. How to add the budget to ChatGPT — step by step, with screenshots — is in the guide ["How to connect ChatGPT to your budget"](@/guides/connect-chatgpt/index.en.md).
