A transaction now has a time: you see it in the feed before the account and change it in the card next to the date. Within a day, transactions are ordered by the time of the purchase rather than by when they were entered — transactions from bank SMS line up in the same order as the messages. A transaction for today without a time gets the current one; older transactions took their time from the SMS or from when they were entered that same day.
