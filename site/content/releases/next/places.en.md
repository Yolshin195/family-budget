"Directories" now has "Places" — the shops, cafés and markets where you pay. A place has other spellings (Thai included), a default category and a default account. Quick entry search finds both categories and places: pick 7-Eleven and the Groceries category and your card are filled in, and "Save & add another" keeps the place for the next expense. The full transaction form has a "Place" field, and "Transactions" shows where the money went. Claude records spending at places too, and adds new places only with your consent and the "Places: add and edit" permission. More in the guide ["Places: shops, cafés and markets"](@/guides/places/index.en.md).
