[extra]

If you set a monthly limit after a weekly one, or pressed "Remove limit from date" with the "Month" period, the Overview kept counting the old weekly limit. Now the later rule replaces the earlier one from the start of its own period — in the Overview, on the "Spending limits" page and for the assistant — while past periods keep their old limits.
