A receipt item now has a name, and the "× price" button opens "Quantity", "Unit" (pcs, kg, l, g), "Price" and "Discount" — the app works out the item amount itself: 2 × 25 − 3 = 47. "More" holds the "Receipt discount", split across the items. In the list a receipt is shown by category with the goods under each. The quick entry button is now called "Full details", and quick entry itself hasn't changed: amount, category, Enter. Claude can also record receipts with prices over MCP and edit single items.
