The balance from a bank SMS (“Outstanding Balance”) is now kept on the account. The card on the “Accounts” page shows “At the bank” with the SMS time and either “Matches” or the difference from your transactions up to that time; the “Reconcile balance” link fills in the SMS balance. The same check sits above the statement feed. Claude sees the difference in balances and helps find a missing or extra transaction.
