Today a project member and a person without an account have different records in “People”. If Pinpan grows up and joins the family budget by invitation, she gets a new member record, while years of purchases “For Pinpan” stay with the old one.

## The idea: link the records

The project owner will be able to say that a new member is a person already in “People”. The earlier record becomes the member's record: the name, other names and all the purchases stay, and the extra record is archived. The “For whom” filter shows all purchases for that person — both before and after joining.

## What we're still deciding

- Whether to offer linking right when someone joins by invitation, or only by hand in “People”.
- Whether linked records can be unlinked if they were linked by mistake.
