Today you can write to us in GitHub Issues — which is not convenient for everyone. We want to add:

- **a review form** on the [Reviews](@/feedback.en.md) page: you will be able to write without a GitHub account, and the reviews you allow us to publish will appear there in three languages;
- **comments under blog posts** — to discuss releases, guides and ideas right next to them.

Comments will run on our own server, with no ads and no third-party trackers. The place for them on the site is already in place.
