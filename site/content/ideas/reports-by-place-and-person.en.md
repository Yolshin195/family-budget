Today each transaction shows its place and recipient, and the “For whom” filter in “Transactions” totals a month for one person. You can't yet compare people or places with each other or see how spending changes from month to month.

## The idea: two reports

- **By place** — how much was spent at each place in a period, how many visits and the average receipt. You see which shop eats the grocery budget.
- **By recipient** — how much went on each person and on “For everyone”, by category and by month. You see what a child costs in a year.

Both reports are in the base currency and lead to the transactions behind each amount. Claude will be able to answer the same questions: “how much did we spend on Pinpan this year”.

## What we're still deciding

- Whether to split shared purchases “For everyone” between members or show them as a separate line.
- Whether limits per person are needed — say, for pocket money.
